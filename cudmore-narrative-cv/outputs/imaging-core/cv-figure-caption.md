**Analysis from microscope to publication.** The CloudScope desktop GUI is used for initial inspection, curation, and analysis in real-time during an experiment and on the scope. Then, in the lab, the same desktop GUI is used for detailed curation, analysis, and figure generation. CloudScope GUI saves complete datasets including all raw imaging data and analysis. These datasets are then uploaded to an online archive and are immediately available for viewing on the web with ClouScope-Web as interactive figures.
