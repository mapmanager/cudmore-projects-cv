### Verified core activities

- The Confocal and Electron Microscopy Core provides access, consultation, and
  training in confocal and electron microscopy.
- Its services include quantification and analysis of confocal and electron
  microscopy data, along with training in several commercial image-analysis
  applications.
- Its emerging technologies include correlative light and electron microscopy,
  three-dimensional scanning electron microscopy reconstruction of neurons,
  expansion microscopy, and super-resolution microscopy.
- The Histology and Imaging Core provides self-service access, contracted
  services, consultation, and training in neuroanatomical methods and image
  analysis.
- The Histology and Imaging Core operates a Miltenyi Biotec UltraMicroscope
  Blaze for automated light-sheet imaging of large or multiple cleared samples
  at subcellular resolution.
- The Histology and Imaging Core offers brain clearing and light-sheet
  microscopy services and is developing emerging light-sheet methods.
- The Histology and Imaging Core provides image-analysis workstations with
  Imaris and Dragonfly.
- Core activities verified from official NIDA IRP pages on August 30, 2026.

### Verified core activities

- The facility serves University of Maryland Baltimore researchers and
  extramural users.
- Its mission includes high-resolution in vivo and in vitro imaging, researcher
  training, optimization of data acquisition, image processing, and extraction
  of relevant data features.
- Supported experiments include fixed samples, cultured cells, organ slices,
  and small animals.
- Available methods include multicolor imaging, multiphoton in vivo imaging,
  fluorescence resonance energy transfer, fluorescence recovery after
  photobleaching, photoactivation, and uncaging.
- A Zeiss LS7 light-sheet microscope supports large two-dimensional and
  three-dimensional samples, cleared and water-based preparations, and
  mesoscale specimens.
- Light-sheet analysis software includes Zeiss, Arivis, and Imaris.
- Additional instruments support stimulated emission depletion
  super-resolution microscopy, fast live-cell imaging, long-term incubated
  imaging, tiling, stitching, and reconstruction.
- Core activities verified from the official facility page on August 30, 2026.
