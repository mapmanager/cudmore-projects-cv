## Role Priorities

VivoSense develops end-to-end digital-health products that connect wearable and physiological sensors, scientific algorithms, and production software for human trials. The position is responsible for evaluating and onboarding new sensors and data sources, assessing SDKs and APIs, understanding sampling and device behavior, and investigating signal-quality and data-pipeline problems.

The role also requires prototyping analytical methods with Python and Jupyter, translating scientific methods into technical specifications and reference implementations, and defining data models, interfaces, expected outputs, acceptance criteria, and validation requirements. The engineer works across Science, Data Science, Product, Quality, and Engineering to move analytical workflows into production and document their behavior and limitations.

Relevant signals named in the posting include accelerometry, PPG, ECG, heart rate, HRV, temperature, sleep, and respiration. Cloud platforms and distributed processing are preferred. Regulated healthcare, clinical research, and medical-device development are listed as additional experience rather than core requirements.

## Evidence to Emphasize

- Custom microscopy and electrophysiology acquisition systems demonstrate integration of modular hardware with real-time acquisition, visualization, storage, and scientific analysis.
- PiE demonstrates distributed acquisition, device coordination, timing-sensitive microcontroller integration, environmental sensing, live video, remote monitoring, structured event records, and transfer of completed data.
- SanPy provides event detection and feature extraction for physiological recordings. AcqStore includes time-series and image-derived measurements such as heart-rate estimation, blood-flow velocity, diameter, and intensity events.
- SanPy and AcqStore retain raw data, parameters, results, quality information, and individual calculation failures so results can be inspected and curated.
- Python, Jupyter, NumPy, SciPy, and pandas support algorithm development, examples, and documentation. Documented APIs and schemas translate scientific methods into interfaces that applications and engineers can reuse.
- Zarr-based outputs and the CloudScope-Web and SanPy-Web applications demonstrate movement from analysis backends to interactive presentation of completed results.
- Instructional laboratories in ECG/EKG, pulse oximetry, accelerometer-based activity measurement, distributed sensors, and internet dashboards establish direct engagement with physiological measurement concepts.
- Grant-supported multidisciplinary leadership demonstrates continued implementation, architectural direction, coordination, and mentorship across engineering and biology.
