## Return to Baltimore

I recently left my Assistant Professor position at UC Davis on good terms and
returned to Baltimore, where my wife and I have our home. We had originally
hoped to relocate west together, but her career remains firmly based in
Baltimore, and returning was the right decision for us. My position at UC Davis
was supported entirely through external funding and did not include startup
support. Although I secured multiple grants, that funding model did not provide
a sustainable foundation for the research program I wanted to continue
building. I am now looking forward to contributing my scientific and
software-development experience within the Baltimore research community.
